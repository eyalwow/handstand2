# MOVALYT illustration review

Visual audit of all 23 images in Draft PR #37, 2026-11-04. Twenty images were inspected from GitHub. Three larger files (Advanced Tuck Planche, Handstand and the L-Sit board) were inspected from the source ZIP after their Git blob IDs and SHA-256 hashes matched the branch and manifest respectively.

Review checks cover visible joint positions, support and silhouette, both printed titles, and the MOVALYT style (faceless muscular grayscale figure, black shorts and backdrop, lime restricted to graphics or the assistance band). Image overlap and foreshortening can obscure joints. Treat the tuck progression names as MOVALYT labels and distinguish the levels by progressively opening the hips while keeping the lever or planche torso near horizontal. The [World Gymnastics floor elements](https://www.gymnastics.sport/publicdir/rules/files/en_1.1%20-%20MAG%20Code%20of%20Points%202025-2028.pdf) provide a visual reference for full and straddle planche.

The inventory folders and manifest record the original handoff. This review records proposed dispositions separately. Five of the nine source-approved exports are visually credible as delivered, and four merit another check. The source reports an explicit selection for Banded Full Front Lever, positive feedback for Advanced Tuck Front Lever, and approval after correction for One-Leg Planche. Moving to the next exercise or mentioning multiple versions is weaker evidence of selection. The original selection turns and exact selected images were unavailable.
