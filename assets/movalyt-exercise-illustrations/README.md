# MOVALYT exercise illustrations

23 standalone exercise illustrations in `ready/`, each visually reviewed after edits. Six valid originals retain their exact bytes; 17 edited or newly created exports resolve the recorded issues. All tiles have one athlete/exercise, English and Hebrew titles, a faceless muscular grayscale character with black curly hair/shorts, black background, and lime limited to decoration or assistance bands.

`manifest.csv` is the current integration inventory: exactly 23 `ready` records with bilingual titles, paths, format/dimensions, byte/pixel SHA-256, original selection status, source hash/path, edit method and individual visual findings. `ready` means passed this visual asset review, not a new historical user selection. Formats and aspect ratios remain native; do not stretch them. No app integration is performed here.

## Preserved originals

Seventeen superseded/problematic originals are retained byte-for-byte under `archive/`, with their own `archive/manifest.csv`. They are excluded from the ready count and must not be integrated. The highlighted Tuck L-Sit variant is archived rather than recolored; the valid grayscale Tuck L-Sit remains unchanged. The multi-exercise boards and Muscle-Up sequence are archived and replaced by standalone L-Sit, One-Leg L-Sit and Muscle-Up tiles. A real Tuck Planche is supplied separately.

`provenance/source-README.txt` and `source-manifest.csv` are the unchanged ZIP documents; `handoff-manifest.csv` and `initial-VISUAL-REVIEW.md` preserve the pre-fix records. Historical paths in those snapshots are evidence of the original handoff; use current manifests to locate assets. Original source approvals are provenance, not current review results.

## Review and scope

See [VISUAL-REVIEW.md](VISUAL-REVIEW.md) for per-image results and [FIX-REPORT.md](FIX-REPORT.md) for changes, validation and remaining limitations. Images were edited using the built-in image generation tool with local references, not an external paid provider. No application code, integration, merge or deployment is included. All files are confined to this assets directory.
